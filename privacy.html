Privacy policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

Buy From Here is a small site that shows fashion from other brands. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. We keep it to the minimum the site needs to work.

What we collect

If you just browse, we don't ask for anything. When you tap a Buy button, we record which product and brand you clicked and when, so we can see which pieces people like. That record isn't tied to you unless you're signed in.

If you sign in, we keep your email address and, if you use Google, the name and email Google shares with us. We also keep the pieces you save and the brands and categories you follow, so they're there on any device.

Your browser also remembers a few small things on your own device, like your sign-in and the last email you used, so you don't have to retype it.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with advertisers. We never see your payment details, because you buy on the brand's own website, not here.

Who helps us run the site

Our database and sign-in run on Supabase. Product photos may be resized through Cloudinary. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Sign-in emails are sent through Google's email service. These services only handle your data to run the site for us.

When you click Buy, you leave this site. The brand's website and the affiliate network that tracks the sale (for example Awin, CJ, Impact or Rakuten) may use cookies on their side so the sale can be credited to us. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, we only ask Google for your name, email address and profile picture link, and we only use them to sign you in and greet you. We don't access anything else in your Google account. Our use of information from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Keeping it, and deleting it

We keep your account until you ask us to delete it. Email kasra.rasoulian@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and we'll delete your account, your saved pieces and your follows, usually within a few days. You can also ask us for a copy of what we hold about you.

Where we are

Buy From Here is run from Vancouver, Canada, for shoppers in Canada and the US. Some of the services above store data in the United States.

Changes

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
